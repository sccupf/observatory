Methodological architecture

Evidence first. Interpretation last.

UPF26 keeps source evidence, transcription, deterministic rule firing, classification and human review distinct. Ambiguity is an output state, not a reason to invent a confidence percentage.

1 · Evidence & transcription

Source material and transcribed facts remain traceable so later judgements can be audited back to the underlying evidence.

2 · Deterministic classification

Versioned rules can emit NOVA 1–4, unresolved 3/4, rule conflict or insufficient evidence. Determinative conflicts fail closed.

3 · Blinded independent review

Two reviewers assess the same locked evidence and methodology independently. Reconciliation creates a new record and does not overwrite either review.
