Business collaboration brief

Public, redacted exploration of practical opportunities. No confidential research assets, commercial forecasts or unsupported product claims.

Commercial status: exploration only. The methodology is a DRAFT and the formal scientific pilot has not started. SCC UPF is not currently advertised here as a certified scoring tool, a clinically validated product, a regulatory decision engine or a source of proven commercial returns.
Opportunity A

Auditable evidence infrastructure

Potential collaborations around public-source tracking, source lineage, metadata qualification, archive integrity, version history and corrections.

Candidate deliverable: scoped evidence-dashboard demonstration using publicly shareable datasets.

Opportunity B

Reproducible methods

Potential research-tooling work using explicit classification rule versions, structured observations, blinded-review workflows and reproducible reports.

Candidate deliverable: methodology demonstrator, subject to scope, legal terms and scientific scrutiny.

Opportunity C

Evidence literacy and reporting

Explainers that distinguish processing classification, nutrient composition, exposure assumptions, observational association and causal claims.

Candidate deliverable: a transparently sourced workshop or public methods briefing, not individual dietary advice.
