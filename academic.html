Protocol question and test design

How much information is lost when a packaged food is represented only by its NOVA class instead of independently described processing, whole-food retention, formulation, nutrition and exposure?

The proposed test uses 20 adversarial cases, two independent blinded reviewers and a frozen release. Case 0001 (the motivating coated-peanut example) is excluded from prospective endpoints because it informed the design.

Methods safeguards

  • Evidence → transcription → observation → rule firing → classification → independent reviews → reconciliation → interpretation.
  • Permitted unresolved and insufficient-evidence outcomes.
  • No collapsed “confidence score”; uncertainty is shown by component.
  • Nutrition and exposure descriptors cannot change a NOVA classification.
  • Changes after a prospective endpoint is locked require a new version and, where needed, a fresh pilot.

What can be evaluated now?

Researchers may critique the public protocol design, versioned evidence stream, redaction policy, endpoint definitions and stop/go criteria.

Complete reproducibility and formal manuscript review still require a frozen release and an authorised evidence package. Private data are not silently exposed for convenience.
